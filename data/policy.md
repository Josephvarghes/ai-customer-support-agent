# AI Customer Support Agent - Refund Policy

This document outlines the strict, rigid refund rules that the agent must enforce during evaluations.

## 1. General Refund Window
* **30-Day Limit**: All refund requests must be made within **30 days** of the purchase date. Any purchase made more than 30 days ago is strictly non-refundable.

## 2. Product Condition Rules by Category
* **Electronics**:
  * Can be refunded in either **sealed** or **opened** condition.
  * If the condition is **opened**, a 15% restocking fee is deducted from the refund amount, unless the item is reported as defective.
* **Apparel**:
  * Can only be refunded if the condition is **sealed** (unworn, original packaging/tags intact).
  * If the condition is **opened**, the refund is strictly denied.
* **Cosmetics**:
  * Can only be refunded if the condition is **sealed** (due to hygiene regulations).
  * If the condition is **opened**, the refund is strictly denied.

## 3. Refund Limits & Membership Caps
* **Standard Tier**: Standard membership tier accounts are capped at a maximum refund of **$200.00** per transaction. Any refund request for an item costing more than $200.00 will be denied.
* **Gold Tier**: No refund cap.
* **Premium Tier**: No refund cap.

## 4. Non-Refundable Items
* **Clearance Items**: Any items purchased on clearance (indicated by prices ending in `.99` or explicit clearance markings) are non-refundable.
* **Gift Cards**: Gift card purchases are strictly non-refundable.
